# Enclave 16: Strategic Corridor Depth, Cognitive Resonance & Dynamic 3D Polish

Transform the infinite 3D Enclave Corridor from a uniform sequence of doors into a high-tension, deeply rewarding hard-science narrative simulation with strategic door archetypes, psychometric skill checks, dramatic chamber aftermaths, unlockable Grand Project powers, and an adaptive 3D mobile HUD.

### User Review & Critical Decisions

> [!IMPORTANT]
> All core product and design choices have been confirmed from your answers and are integrated into this end-to-end blueprint:

- **Confirmed Decision 1 — Tiered Corridor Door Archetypes**: The infinite hallway will spawn four distinct door classes with unique 3D visual signatures and strategic rules: **Standard Lab Bays**, **Cognitive-Locked Vaults** (requiring specific MBTI cognitive functions or Grand Project thresholds), **Ticking Crisis Bulkheads** (with urgent relay countdowns and penalty strobes), and **Sanctuary Lounge Alcoves** (for sibling bonding and Vex stress relief).
- **Confirmed Decision 2 — Cognitive Resonance Checks & Immediate Dramatic Aftermath**: Choices inside a chamber door calculate a live **Cognitive Resonance Match %** based on the active genius's MBTI stack, Big 5 OCEAN profile, and Vex state, transitioning directly into an in-room **Dramatic Aftermath Screen** with partner dialogue reactions, breakthrough bonuses, and visible environmental consequences before stepping back into the hallway.
- **Confirmed Decision 3 — Auto-Slimming Mobile HUD, Dynamic 3D Corridor Atmosphere & Project Milestone Powers**: The HUD automatically slims down into a minimal compass ticker while walking so the 3D corridor fills the screen; the 3D hallway dynamically renders state-driven lighting and bioluminescent/spark particles; and crossing **50% and 100% on any of the 8 Grand Projects** unlocks active/passive gameplay abilities.

### 1. Overview & Core Concept

- **What It Does**: Elevates **Enclave 16** into a cohesive, high-replayability 3D narrative strategy game. Players explore an infinite enclosed 3D corridor where every door presents a distinct tactical challenge—encouraging players to scout ahead down the hallway, leave locked or high-risk doors, switch Rashomon POV to the right genius, defuse ticking crises before the Nightly Grand Feast, and unlock colony-wide scientific superpowers.
- **Target Audience / Persona**: Fans of deep choice-driven narrative games (*Choice of Games*, *Citizen Sleeper*, *Disco Elysium*) who want spatial 3D immersion, hard-science puzzle-solving, and authentic character psychology on mobile and desktop.
- **Key Value**: Replaces uniform button-clicking with meaningful risk/reward tension, emotional payoff after every choice, and tangible progression rewards.

### 2. User Experience & Visual Design

- **Key User Flows**:
  1. **Corridor Scouting & Auto-Slimming Walk Mode**: As the player moves with the thumbstick or `WASD`, the top narrative card smoothly collapses into a sleek 1-line **Corridor Telemetry Ticker** (`BAY 04 AHEAD • SILAS [ISTJ] • 22m`), giving the 3D corridor over 85% of the viewport. Stopping near a door gently expands its chamber preview badge.
  2. **Approaching Differentiated 3D Doors**:
     - **Standard Lab Bay**: Cyan/Amber squad bulkhead frame with sliding blast doors.
     - **Cognitive-Locked Vault (`🔒 VAULT`)**: Violet/Gold geometric containment seal displaying its lock requirement (e.g., `REQUIRES Ti / Ni OR FIRST FORGE ≥ 50%`). Players can step back into the hall, switch to a matching Genius via the Rashomon selector (or use a unlocked Forge Override perk), and re-enter.
     - **Ticking Crisis Bulkhead (`⚠ CRISIS • 2 RELAYS`)**: Pulsing crimson hazard beacon and 3D warning strobes on the corridor wall. If ignored for too many relays, it triggers a colony morale and magnetic flux penalty at the Nightly Grand Feast.
     - **Sanctuary Lounge Alcove (`🍵 SANCTUARY`)**: Warm bioluminescent emerald/gold glow where two Enclave siblings share a quiet found-family moment, venting `-25% Vex Stress` and granting a **Sibling Synergy Buff** for the next chamber.
  3. **Chamber Decision Console with Cognitive Resonance**:
     - Each choice card displays a **Cognitive Resonance Badge** (`94% SYNERGY • BREAKTHROUGH CHANCE` vs. `48% STRAIN • OFF-STACK RISK`) computed from the active character's MBTI stack (`Si-Te`, `Ne-Fi`, `Ti-Se`, etc.), OCEAN traits, and Grand Project perks.
  4. **Dramatic Aftermath Screen (In-Room Payoff)**:
     - Executing a choice transitions the modal to a rich **Chamber Aftermath View** rather than closing abruptly:
       - **Outcome Tier Banner**: *Cognitive Breakthrough (+50% Bonus)*, *Clean Resolution*, or *High-Strain Resolution*.
       - **Partner Reaction Quote**: Immediate in-character banter from the room's partner genius, validated by the Psychometric Anti-Drift Guardrail.
       - **Live Delta Chips & Milestone Alert**: Visual badges showing Project progress gained, Vex stress shift, Morale change, and any newly unlocked **Grand Project Milestone Power (50% / 100%)**.
       - **"Return to Hallway" Action**: Steps back into the 3D corridor as the door's physical blast panels seal with a `RESOLVED ✓` hologram.

- **Visual Identity & Theme**:
  - *Aesthetic Direction*: **Bioluminescent Solarpunk Observatory meets Precision Brass Telemetry**—deep obsidian glass surfaces, crisp technical monospaced telemetry badges, and reactive 3D corridor lighting.
  - *Color Palette & Mood*:
    - Dominant Background: `ObsidianNight (#060B12)` & `ConsoleGlass (#CC0A1320)`
    - Primary Accent: `LuciferinCyan (#00F5D4)` (Wilds / Telemetry / Active Navigation)
    - Secondary Accents: `ForgeAmber (#FF9F1C)` (Forge / Industrial), `SimulacrumViolet (#9B5DE5)` (Mind / Cognitive Vaults), `BrassGold (#D4AF37)` (Hub / Milestones / Sanctuary), `VexCrimson (#FF3366)` (Ticking Crisis / Vex Override).
  - *Typography & Hierarchy*: Bold geometric headers paired with high-contrast tabular numerals for Resonance %, hallway depth meters, and project milestones.
  - *Interactive Feedback & Motion*:
    - **Dynamic 3D Hallway Atmosphere**: Floating bioluminescent spore particles when Colony Morale / Wilds projects are high; pulsing crimson bulkhead emergency strips when a Ticking Crisis Door is active or Vex stress exceeds 75%; golden cymatic floor ripples when a Grand Project milestone is unlocked.

### 3. Key Product Decisions & Trade-Offs

- **Decision 1: Soft-Gating vs. Hard-Gating Cognitive Vault Doors**
  - *Chosen Approach*: **Tactical Soft-Gate with Bypass Options**. If the player enters a Cognitive-Locked Vault without the matching MBTI function (e.g., `Ti`/`Ni`) or required Project level, they can either: (a) step back into the hallway and switch POV to the right Genius for free, (b) use an unlocked **First Forge 50% Override Perk**, or (c) force the door open at a heavy `+18% Vex Stress` cost.
  - *Why*: Encourages strategic use of the 16-character roster and hallway exploration without ever trapping the player in a dead end.
- **Decision 2: In-Modal Dramatic Aftermath vs. Instant Modal Close**
  - *Chosen Approach*: **Two-Stage Chamber Modal (Decision Stage → Dramatic Aftermath Stage)**.
  - *Why*: Gives every decision emotional weight, lets players read the consequence narrative and partner banter at their own pace, and highlights Breakthrough bonuses before returning to 3D movement.
- **Decision 3: Active & Passive Grand Project Milestone Powers (50% & 100%)**
  - *Chosen Approach*: Crossing `50%` and `100%` on each of the 8 Grand Projects unlocks permanent Enclave capabilities (e.g., *First Forge*: Vault Override; *Stormbreaker*: Crisis Defusal Shield; *Sanctuary*: Spore Morale Aura; *Genesis Garden*: Sanctuary Healing Boost; *Lyceum*: +15% Cognitive Resonance across all minds; *Simulacrum*: Exact Outcome Preview; *Living Atlas*: 4-Bay Radar Foresight; *Obsessive Table*: Double Feast Stress Relief).
  - *Why*: Transforms the 8 Grand Projects from passive progress bars into an interconnected tech tree that rewards long-term play.

- **Data Model & State Extensions**:
  - `DoorArchetype` enum (`STANDARD_LAB`, `COGNITIVE_VAULT`, `TICKING_CRISIS`, `SANCTUARY_LOUNGE`) added to `CorridorDoor3D`, along with `requiredCognitiveFunction: String?`, `requiredProjectId: String?`, `requiredProjectPercent: Int`, and `crisisTurnsRemaining: Int?`.
  - `ChoiceResonanceResult` model holding `resonancePercent: Int`, `tierLabel: String` (`BREAKTHROUGH`, `RESONANT`, `STRAINED`), `bonusProjectDelta: Int`, `bonusStressDelta: Int`, and `partnerReactionQuote: String`.
  - `ChamberAftermathState` in `EnclaveUiState` so resolving a choice transitions the open `ChamberDoorDecisionModal` into its Aftermath view with full telemetry deltas and newly unlocked Project Milestones before closing.
  - `ProjectMilestonePerk` definitions for all 8 Grand Projects at 50% and 100%, surfaced in both the Chamber Modal and the Living Atlas & Projects Drawer.

- **Interactive Component & State Mapping**:
  - **Walking Detection (`isPlayerMoving`)**: Tracked in `MainActivity` / `EnclaveWorld3DCanvas` from thumbstick and keyboard vectors; smoothly animates the top narrative panel into a compact 1-line HUD bar while moving.
  - **Crisis Countdown Tick**: Resolving any door decrements `crisisTurnsRemaining` on active unvisited `TICKING_CRISIS` doors behind or near the player; defusing a Crisis Door grants a Heroic Morale & Project bonus, while letting it expire triggers a Feast hazard report.
  - **Sanctuary Lounge Interaction**: Entering a `SANCTUARY_LOUNGE` door offers restorative sibling interactions that clear `-25%` Vex Stress, restore Colony Morale, and grant a `+15% Cognitive Resonance` boost on the next chamber.
  - **Persistence Compatibility**: All new door archetype fields, crisis timers, and unlocked perks serialize cleanly in `EnclaveSaveSerializer` for both Guest JSON save files and Firestore cloud sync.
